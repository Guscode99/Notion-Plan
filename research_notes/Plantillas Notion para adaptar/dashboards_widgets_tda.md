## Q1. What are the most popular student dashboard templates with widgets and horizontal layouts in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
Three groups of templates come up again and again. The first is a set of **established creator dashboards**: Easlo's free Student Dashboard (about 4.9k Gumroad ratings), Gridfiti's paid **Student OS** ($19) and Notion Everything's **Notion Student Dashboard** ($5, works on the Free plan). The second is **aesthetic dashboards built around widgets** that the 2026 roundups recommend (Aura Gradient, Colorful Dashboard, Aesthetic Student Planner, Janice Studies, and University Hub by Emma Studies, which has a spaced-repetition section). The third is **Etsy best-sellers** with thousands of sales. Several **law-specific templates** also exist (Law School Planner, Simran Nanda's Law Student Template, Altitude's Law School Reading Planner). These are good structural starting points but are not widget-heavy.

## Q2. What layout conventions do the best ones share?

### Takeaway
The common pattern is:
- a **cover image and icon** header;
- a **row of embedded widgets** (clock or time, weather, quote, pomodoro), often split left/right;
- **callout "zones" in columns** for navigation links (a sidebar-style nav column);
- **2–3 content columns** of linked database views (tasks, upcoming deadlines, calendar);
- **course galleries** with covers;
- toggles or tabs to hide anything used less often.

As of March 2026, Notion's native **tabs block** and **dashboard view** formalize this pattern, but the dashboard view is Business/Enterprise only.

### Cited Findings
- **Widgets split left and right:** Aesthetic Student Planner puts four interactive widgets on the left and weather and time on the right. — [Notionplanners](https://notionplanners.com/free-aesthetic-notion-templates/) (via search summary)
- **Sidebar navigation** is a listed feature of Easlo's Student Dashboard ("quick sidebar navigation"). — [Easlo](https://www.easlo.co/templates/student-dashboard)
- **Columns:** created with a Column block or by dragging one block beside another until a blue guide appears. Linked database blocks cannot be dragged straight into columns; Gridfiti describes a workaround. Full-width mode is a page setting; custom widths need extensions such as Notion Enhancer or Stylish (advanced users only). — [Gridfiti, how to make Notion aesthetic](https://gridfiti.com/how-to-make-notion-aesthetic/) (via search summary)
- Notion VIP's rule of thumb: "**Use columns when you can; enable full-width when you must.**" It also recommends **callouts arranged in columns for high-priority links**, a wiki-style structure of linked pages, toggles to hide content used less often, negative space as a core principle, and **multiple filtered views of one database** rather than duplicate linked databases. — [Notion VIP](https://www.notion.vip/insights/design-usable-delightful-notion-pages) (via search summary)
- Wrapping related blocks in **callouts to make visual zones** is an "instant cleanup". Large patterned images work well as headers, and GIF stickers can add accents. — [UX Collective Bootcamp](https://bootcamp.uxdesign.cc/5-ways-to-instantly-make-your-notion-dashboard-template-look-better-more-aesthetic-7a119ef0be01) (via search summary)
- **Native tabs block** (release notes, March 26, 2026): organizes content into "clean, clickable sections, without the maze of subpages". One guide says any tab can be set to a Dashboard layout. — [Notion releases 2026-03-26](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-03-26); [Kurashi Notion guide](https://kurashi-notion.com/en/blogs/notion/dashboard) (via search summary)
- **Native dashboard view** (announced March 10, 2026):
  - It is a database view whose widgets can be tables, boards, charts or calendars, pulling from several databases.
  - Reported limits are up to **4 widgets per row and 12 total**, with a **single global filter**, and no freeform text, callouts or toggles between views.
  - It is reported as limited to **Business and Enterprise** plans.
  — [Notion releases 2026-03-10](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-03-10); [Matthias Frank](https://matthiasfrank.de/en/notion-dashboards-database-view/); [DEV Community](https://dev.to/benarikutai/three-ways-to-get-a-notion-dashboard-in-2026-20g7) (via search summary; the sources disagree on some limits)
- **Theming:** Student OS ships an "aesthetic" and a "minimal" theme, so creators treat a professional/minimal look and a pastel/aesthetic look as two skins on the same structure. — [Gridfiti shop](https://shop.gridfiti.com/products/notion-student-os-template)

### Inferences
- The "world-standard" horizontal dashboard Carlos wants matches what the sources describe:
  - Row 1: a cover, then a widget row of 3–4 embeds (clock, pomodoro, countdown, weather or progress).
  - Row 2: three columns. Left is a narrow navigation column of callout links (courses, notes, legal norms, review). Centre is Today's tasks, kept short, plus a Kanban. Right is upcoming deadlines and the calendar.
  - Row 3: a course gallery with covers.
  - Tabs replace long vertical scrolling.
- On Free or Plus, this has to be built with columns, callouts and the tabs block, because the native dashboard view is Business/Enterprise only (as reported).
- A professional look is mainly about restraint: a minimal theme, few colors, callout zones and whitespace. These same guidelines appear in the ADHD calm-design advice (Q3).

### Gaps
- Whether the tabs block is available on the Free plan was not confirmed.
- The exact limits of the dashboard view on each plan conflict between sources; check https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-03-10.
- No source gave a measured comparison (for example, a survey) of which layout is "most common". The conventions above come from creator guides and template descriptions.

### Inferences
- Features that fit Carlos and involve no gamification:
  - a single **Inbox / brain-dump** database captured from anywhere;
  - a dashboard block for **"Today: one main focus + max 3 tasks"**;
  - **"next tiny step" and "energy/time" properties** on tasks;
  - **toggles or tabs** to hide everything outside the current context;
  - an **always-visible pomodoro embed**;
  - a **calm/minimal theme** rather than "dopamine" colors.
- Gigi's planner and NotoMantra's ADHD Life OS have the most credible social proof. Both are life planners rather than academic systems, so Carlos would borrow their focus and capture patterns, not their structure.
- Gamification is not universal in ADHD templates. Most of the listed ones lean on capture, focus and reducing visual load instead. Watch for "missions", streak or "reward" framing, as in Ultimate ADHD Life Planner's "daily focus missions".

### Inferences
- For Carlos's always-visible pomodoro, the safest free choices are **Flocus or Pomofocus embedded via /embed**, plus Indify for a clock, countdown or weather within its 6-widget free cap. Self-hosted HTML widgets avoid third-party risk entirely (the project tasks note such widgets were already built).
- WidgetBox's reported shutdown shows the main risk of third-party widgets: an embed can silently break. A dashboard should still work if any one widget disappears.
- On Free or Plus, progress bars are better built from **formula properties** (native and unlimited) than from charts (limited to one on Free). The dashboard view would need a Business plan.
